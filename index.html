How it started?

As we all know, 2020 was a year the world was striving to survive the global pandemic of covid-19. During these dire times we felt an urge to make an impact with whatever we had, and so we tried!!

We tried to uplift the underprivileged and help the needy with all our resources and material we could arrange, it was tough but we didn't lose our hope and kept going in order to bring a change everyone was expecting the bigger authorities to bring in the societies on smaller.
